Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026 · Effective: August 1, 2026

The short version: HalfPint collects only what it needs to work. We don't sell your data, show you ads, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your drinking habits are your business — we just help you understand them.

Who we are

HalfPint is a drinking moderation app built by Grain Studio. Our registered contact is pint@gethalfpint.com.

This policy applies to the HalfPint iOS app and the gethalfpint.com website.

What we collect

We collect the minimum information needed to make HalfPint work:

Data type What it includes Why we collect it
Email address Your email used to sign up Account authentication and transactional emails
User ID A unique identifier for your account To link your data across sessions and devices
Drink logs Drink type, quantity, date, cost To calculate your savings and track your progress
Mood data Optional morning mood check-ins To show patterns between mood and drinking
Profile data Name, date of birth (optional), preferences To personalise Pint's messages and insights
Product interaction How you use the app (screens visited, features used) To improve the app experience
Purchase history Pro subscription status To unlock Pro features you've paid for

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect your location
  • We don't access your contacts
  • We don't read your messages
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites
  • We don't collect health or medical data
  • We don't use your data for advertising

How we use your data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • Calculate and display your savings, life gained, and streak
  • Send transactional emails (confirmation, password reset, invites)
  • Personalise Pint's messages based on your progress
  • Restore your purchases across devices
  • Improve the app based on how it's used

Who we share data with

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate HalfPint:

Service Purpose Data shared
Supabase Database and authentication Account data, drink logs, profile
RevenueCat Subscription management User ID, purchase history
Resend Transactional email delivery Email address
Apple Sign In with Apple, App Store Apple ID token (not stored)
Google Sign In with Google Google ID token (not stored)

We do not sell your data to any third party. Ever.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is deleted from our systems within 30 days.

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing pint@gethalfpint.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to certain uses of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at pint@gethalfpint.com.

Children's privacy

HalfPint is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will delete it immediately.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS), secure authentication via Supabase, and row-level security on our database.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you discover a security vulnerability, please contact us at pint@gethalfpint.com.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll notify you via email or an in-app notice. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

Questions?

We're a small team and we read every email.
