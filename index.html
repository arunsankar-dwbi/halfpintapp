🍺 Pint says

"We built HalfPint to help you drink less and gain more. Your data helps us do that — nothing else. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we never will. That's not a legal formality. It's how we're built."

Who we are

HalfPint is a drinking moderation app built by Arun Sankar, trading as Grain ("we", "us", "our"). HalfPint helps you track your drinking habits, understand your patterns, and cut back — on your own terms.

You can reach us at: arunsankar.apps@gmail.com

What we collect

We only collect what we need to make HalfPint work for you.

Data Why we collect it Stored where
Email address Account creation and sign-in Supabase (secure)
Tab name Personalising Pint's voice Supabase (secure)
Drink logs Tracking your progress and calculating gains Supabase (secure)
Mood logs Morning mood check-ins and pattern insights Supabase (secure)

What we never do

✅ We never sell your data. We never show ads. We never share your drinking data with third parties. Ever.

  • We do not sell your personal data to anyone
  • We do not show advertisements
  • We do not share your drinking habits with employers, insurers, or any third party
  • We do not use your data to build advertising profiles
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites
  • We do not use your location
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or any other device data

How we use your data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Show you your drinking patterns, streaks, and savings
  • Personalise Pint's messages and insights
  • Calculate your money saved, calories saved, and life minutes gained
  • Send you optional push notifications (only if you enable them)
  • Process your subscription through Apple's App Store
  • Sync your data across your devices (account users only)

Your rights

You have full control over your data.

Access your data

Export all your data as a CSV from Settings → Privacy & Data → Export my data (Pro feature)

Delete your data

Delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Delete account. Permanent and immediate.

Correct your data

Edit your profile, preferences, and logged data directly within the app at any time.

Use without an account

Use HalfPint entirely locally — no account, no cloud storage. Your data never leaves your device.

Withdraw consent

Delete your account at any time. No questions asked. No retention period beyond what's legally required.

Data portability

Export your data in CSV format to take it wherever you like.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

  • All drink logs are permanently deleted immediately
  • Your profile is permanently deleted immediately
  • Your authentication record is permanently deleted immediately
  • Subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat per their own policies (for legal and financial compliance)

We do not retain your personal data after account deletion.

Children

HalfPint is a drinking moderation app intended for adults aged 18 and over (or the legal drinking age in your country, whichever is higher). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS in transit, encrypted at rest via Supabase). We use Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure your data is only accessible to you. No HalfPint employee can read your drink logs or mood data.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this privacy policy, we will notify you via the app before the changes take effect. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

Continuing to use HalfPint after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
